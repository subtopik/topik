A custom component whose required parent is named `tab`, `step` or `codeTab` gets the general `-parent-required` message, which names no built-in component. The three messages it used to get are gone: the built-in tab, step and code tab report `topik-tabs-parent-required`, `topik-steps-parent-required` and `topik-code-group-parent-required`.
